Editing at the seam

The seam is the first character of the tail. Backspace, Delete, and a selection that reaches the seam are owned by the editor: each removes exactly its range, and the pass re-cuts so the page refills from the tail. Enter splits the paragraph there.

View

Print from this button, not the browser's page print: it prints the editor frame at the page measure. Printing the workbench page would print this panel and the frame's viewport, which is not the document.
